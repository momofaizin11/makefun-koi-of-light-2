# Notes

Concept: a koi made of light rather than a photographed fish. The whole idea is
the contrast - a warm gold body that carries its own glow, hanging on the
diagonal through cold blue-black water.

Palette: amber-gold body (#FDDB9E core, #EAC590 glow, #C67438 wash), deep
indigo water (#262F3E at the top, going blacker downwards), pale blue caustics,
trails and bubble rings, and a cream rim (#FFF0CE) that lifts the fish off the
dark.

What makes it the coolest art: the body is translucent, so the water reads
through it; the fins grow off the spine as ribbons and dissolve from crisp silk
at the root into soft membrane at the tip. The final passes veil the back-fin
rays and spread the scale sheen wide and uneven, so the flanks never turn into a
mechanical rib cage at thumbnail size - the koi stays organic at 300px. The eye
and barbels go on last, above the bloom, so the head reads sharply at any size.
